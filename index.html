Riverside Hospital major incident exercise

A 45-minute simulation, then a 25-minute debrief. The hospital clock runs evenly and fast (2 hospital hours every real minute), from Tuesday 07:00 to Saturday 01:00. The opening message shows in full on the right from the start. Each inject arrives with a sound alert and shows in full in the same panel; the previous message collapses beneath it. Nothing on the projected screen shows how many injects there are or when they come.

Keep this screen off the projector: it lists every inject in advance. Connect the projector after you press Start, or start first and then mirror the display.

When you press Start, a QR code appears in the bottom-left corner. Learners scan it to follow the clock and messages on their phones. Phones keep their own time from the moment you pressed Start, so do not pause or skip during play: those keys are for testing only.
